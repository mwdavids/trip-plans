# Stehekin → Holden Bulger Traverse

- **Peaks:** Sinister, Dome, Dark, Bonanza, Martin (Methow) — 5 Bulger peaks
- **Style:** **4-day** point-to-point, ferry-shuttle-supported, glaciated alpine traverse *(with options — see Plan Variants.)*
- **Region:** Glacier Peak Wilderness + Lake Chelan–Sawtooth Wilderness + Wenatchee NF
- **Approach:** Stehekin via Lady of the Lake → NPS shuttle to High Bridge
- **Exit:** Holden Village → Lucerne shuttle → Lady of the Lake out
- **Party:** 3 — Michael, Kirk, Ethan

### Timing

- **Stehekin → High Bridge shuttle (Stehekin Valley Adventures, summer 2026):** Leaves Landing 8:00 / 11:30 / 2:00 / 5:30. Target **11:30 AM** on Day 1. ~45 min ride → High Bridge ~12:15 PM.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 — Stehekin → Icy Creek saddle high camp (big arrival day)

- **CalTopo:** seg 0 (shuttle, 11.0 mi) + **seg 1** (**9.16 mi** PCT) + approach portion of seg 2 (~2 mi / +4,000 ft Asa Mt push)
- **Route:** **Lady Liberty 8:00 AM** (call to book) → Stehekin 9:30 AM → **11:30 AM SVA shuttle** → **High Bridge ~12:15 PM** → boots-on-trail **~12:30 PM** → **PCT southbound** up West Fork Agnes Creek ~8.6 mi to the **S Fork Agnes log crossing** just south of Swamp Creek camp. Cross the log, push through ~400 vft of slide alder, then ~3,500+ vft on open rocky/heather slopes up the **south side of Asa Mountain** to the ridge crest (~6,400'), then west/SW to **high camp at Icy Creek saddle** (~6,800–7,000 ft, the col south of Gunsight — Riley81's "between Icy Creek and Blue Lake"). Tom Sjolseth’s site is the canonical bivy.
- **Stats:** **~11.2 mi · ~5,800 ft gain · 8–11 hr** on foot (9.16 mi trail + ~2 mi Asa push). Big arrival day, but mostly trail + one sustained climb.
- **Why front-load it:** Frees Day 2 to be a Sinister+Dome combo from high camp (Riley81 template). This is the only way to bag both peaks in a 4-day plan. The alternative — camping at Swamp Creek Day 1, then trying to do Asa push + Sinister + Dome all on Day 2 — is a 16–18 hr push that’s strictly worse than splitting it. Hardrath did Sinister+Dome from a high camp in a single day, but he was 3 weeks deep into continuous climbing; we are starting cold.
- **Ferry timing matters:** Both Lady Liberty (9:30 arrival, 2 hr cushion) and Express (11:00 arrival, 30 min sprint) can catch the 11:30 SVA shuttle. Lady Liberty is the lower-risk play. Express works if NOCA permits are pre-arranged and ferry is on time — any slip and we're on the 2:00 PM shuttle, which forces Variant B (3 PM trail start = no shot at Icy Creek saddle in daylight).
- **Terrain:** Maintained PCT then a log crossing then a short brushy band then 3,500+ ft of open rocky/heather slope hauling overnight + glacier kit + rope. Most of the Asa Mt climb is exposed south-facing — the afternoon-arrival timing means we climb in the worst heat. We'll carry 3 L water capacity per person and fill at S Fork Agnes before the push.
- **Hazards:** S Fork Agnes log crossing (high-water years can be sketchy); heat on the exposed south-facing climb; arriving at camp at dusk if anything goes sideways earlier in the day.
- **Permit:** NOCA backcountry permit needed (covers Stehekin valley camping en route) — pick up at Golden West Visitor Center in Stehekin before shuttle.
- **Camp:** Icy Creek saddle.
- **Bail logic:** From a 12:30 PM trail start, we expect S Fork Agnes log around 4:30–5:30 PM and high camp 9–11 PM. If we're still on the PCT at **5:30 PM** (≈5 hr in, only halfway), bail to **Swamp Creek camp** and reset Day 2 as the Asa push — we're on a de-facto 5-day plan (Variant B) and may need the buffer day.

### Day 2 — Sinister + Dome combo day from high camp

- **CalTopo:** peaks portion of seg 2 (~6–10 mi RT from high camp). Will need redraw.
- **Route (Riley81 2013 template):** Alpine start (alarm 3:30, leave camp ~4:30 with the sun). **Sinister first:** drop SW from camp, traverse west around the Gunsight col, descend onto the **Chickamin Glacier** and bee-line to the **N Face of Sinister**. Climb the N Face: ~1,200 ft at 60°, sustained, one mental picket at the bergschrund, otherwise simul-solo good snow. Summit ~9:30 AM. **Descend Sinister W Ridge** to the Sinister–Dome col (Class 3 + steep snow, no rappel typically needed) — OR if W Ridge looks bad, downclimb the NW Face to col (then a short rap may be needed past a crevasse blocking glacier access). **Dome via Chickamin Glacier:** from col, ascend the glacier west then north, crevasse-navigating around (Riley used deadman anchors on a roped pitch over a steep snow bridge for the final crux). Top out via rocky scramble on the summit block (~100 ft of class 3). Summit Dome ~2:00 PM. **Hard turnaround at 2:00 PM** per Riley. Return same line: downclimb crevasse bridge, back down glacier to col, retrace to high camp. Back at camp ~7:00 PM.
- **Stats:** **~6–10 mi · +5,000–6,000 ft · 12–15 hr camp-to-camp**
- **Terrain:** Steep alpine snow (60° N Face Sinister); heavily crevassed glacier (Chickamin); exposed scrambling; one snow-bridge crossing on Dome that may require belayed pitch + deadman anchors.
- **Crux gear:** 2 pickets, 2 ice screws (Riley never used screws), ice axe + 1 hybrid tool, crampons. Single 60 m rope.
- **Hazards:** Bergschrund at base of Sinister N Face (open by late July most years — if blown, fall back to Sinister W Ridge from the col only, skip the N Face); crevasses on Chickamin (Riley had to rap a crevasse on the Sinister W ridge descent and roped-pitch a snow bridge on Dome); long committing day with no easy bail.
- **Bail:** From high camp — Sinister only (no Dome) if late or schrund is bad. From Sinister summit — skip Dome and return; Dome adds ~5–6 hr to the day. From Dome summit — only way back is the route in.
- **Schrund window:** Mountaineers TR 2023 (late May) found bergschrund at Sinister-Dome col **already opening**. Riley 2013 (July 18) needed only one mental picket. Window for usable bridges: **roughly mid-June to late July**, varies by year. **Late July is the late edge.** Early August may go but is increasingly risky.

### Day 3 — High camp → PCT → Hemlock → Dark + Bonanza → Holden Pass (THE BIG DAY)

- **CalTopo:** descent off Asa Mt back to PCT (reverse of Day 1 finish, ~2 mi / -4,000 ft) + **seg 2.B** Hemlock approach (2.68 mi) + **seg 3** Dark (2.66 mi) + **seg 4** Dark→Bonanza traverse (1.52 mi, the 5.6 crux) + **seg 5** Bonanza→Holden Pass (1.50 mi, Mary Green + rappel)
- **Route:** Pre-dawn strike of high camp. Descend Asa Mt south side back to PCT (steep, loose, slow on descent — Riley81: "unfortunately was just as long and even harder to get down"). Hike PCT south past Spruce Creek to **Hemlock Camp area**. Cut **west into open forest** (**NOT up Swamp Creek — alder hell**) → ~3,500 ft to **Dark NW ridge** → drop to snow → cross **Dark Glacier** → **Dark Peak summit**. Then **traverse the ridge SE to Bonanza** (~6 hr): stay on **right (west) side of ridge** initially (loose/lichen but lower angle); rope up where exposure dictates; simul-climb using horns + micro traxion. **Crux** = west-peak → true-summit Bonanza, sharp/exposed, **one short ~5.6 pitch belayed**. Descend **NE ridge** (scramble) → **east face** (class 3+) → **Mary Green Glacier** (crevassed — rope up) → rappel from **tree anchor at glacier toe slabs (60 m needed)** → down to **Holden Pass**.
- **Stats:** **~12–14 mi · ~6,500–8,000 ft gain · ~16–20 hr** moving — the biggest day of the trip by a wide margin. Includes the 4,000 ft descent off Asa + the 6,500–7,500 ft of Dark/Bonanza work. Party of 3 on the simul ridge + extra rap at Mary Green pushes toward the high end (~18–20 hr).
- **Terrain:** Brutal descent + Class 3 scramble + glacier + exposed alpine ridge + 5.6 pitch + crevassed glacier descent + rappel.
- **Crux gear moments:**
  - Dark→Bonanza simul: light alpine rack, slings on horns, micro traxion for the second
  - Bonanza summit pitch: belayed 5.6 (one short pitch)
  - Mary Green toe-slabs: **60 m rappel** (40 m beta is wrong — confirmed)
- **Water:** Dark Glacier melt + Mary Green melt. None on the ridge traverse — carry 1.5–2 L over.
- **Camp:** Holden Pass. **Bugs are real here** — we'll bring head nets and set up early if we can.
- **⚠️ Realism flag:** This is the make-or-break day for the 4-day plan. If anyone in the party is gassed off Day 2, **invoke the buffer day** (see Plan Variants) and split this into 3a (high camp → Hemlock + Dark, camp on Dark NW ridge or back at Hemlock) and 3b (Bonanza traverse + Mary Green + Holden Pass).
- **Bail options:**
  - Before Dark summit: retreat down the Hemlock Camp open-forest line back to PCT — easiest bail.
  - On the traverse: very few bail lines. Once committed past Dark, going through is generally faster than reversing.
  - From Bonanza summit: standard Mary Green descent is the only sane way down — same as the planned line.

### Day 4 — Martin Peak + exit to Lucerne

- **CalTopo:** **seg 6** Martin Peak (2.59 mi RT from Holden Pass) + **seg 7** Holden Pass→Holden Village (**5.87 mi**). Optional Copper/Fernow tag-on = segs 8 + Optional + 10 (~11.4 mi extra) — skip in base plan.
- **Route:** Holden Pass → **Martin Peak (Methow) West Ridge** (mostly class 3 grass/gully, **one short 4th-class move below summit** — rap anchor exists). Solo-able for a confident party. Return to Holden Pass, descend to **Holden Village** (~3 mi, ~2,500 ft drop on trail). Catch **Holden bus to Lucerne** → ferry south.
- **Stats:** Martin RT from Holden Pass **2.59 mi** · ~2,200 ft gain · 4–5 hr. Holden Pass → Holden Village **5.87 mi** · ~2,500 ft loss · ~2 hr. **Total ~8.5 mi · ~2,200 ft gain · ~2,500 ft loss.**
- **Hazards:** Loose rock in upper west ridge gullies; short 4th class move = bring a 30 m tagline if anyone isn't comfortable downclimbing it.
- **Critical timing:** **We need to know the Holden bus departure before we leave camp.** Set a hard summit turnaround time tied to it. Miss the bus and we're spending the night in Holden.

## Plan variants (pick one)

| Variant | Days | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. 4-day as written above** | 4 | All 5 Bulgers in 4 days; matches Hardrath-style efficiency | Big Day 1 (5,800 ft to high camp from 12:30 PM start) + huge Day 3 (16–20 hr) — needs strong party + good weather + 11:30 SVA shuttle (Lady Liberty 8 AM ferry preferred, Express 8:30 AM as backup) |
| **B. 5-day, split Day 1** | 5 | Camp at Swamp Creek Day 1, do Asa push fresh Day 2 morning; Day 3 peaks; Day 4 monster; Day 5 Martin | Extra day of food + permits; loses the "fast-and-light" feel |
| **C. 5-day, split Day 3** | 5 | Same Day 1–2 as Variant A; split the monster Day 3 into Dark-day + Bonanza-Holden day with a bivy on Dark NW ridge or back at Hemlock | Same total mileage; safest split; **best buffer-day option if Day 1 or 2 went poorly** |
| **D. 4-day, drop Dome** | 4 | Sinister-only from PCT camp via Asa Mt ridge (per Mountaineers 2023 — doable as ~14 hr from PCT camp with light kit, return same day); avoids the high camp; D3/D4 unchanged | Loses Dome; still a brutal day; schrund risk on Sinister N Face |
| **E. 4-day, drop Sinister + Dome** | 4 | Dark + Bonanza + Martin from Stehekin via Hemlock Camp is well-documented | Loses 2 Bulgers; misses the point of starting from Stehekin |

> **Build in 1 extra day of food + flexibility on permits/ferry.** Go in on Variant A. If Day 1 or Day 2 goes long, slide to Variant C (split Day 3). Hardrath-pace requires being a few weeks deep into climbing volume; for a fresh party of 3, the buffer is the smart insurance.

## Gear

**Rope:** 60 m × 8.5–9 mm single. (40 m has been documented as too short for Mary Green toe-slab rappel.)
**Rack:** Light alpine — set of nuts, 4–5 cams (#0.3–#2), 6–8 alpine draws, double-length slings for horns, 1–2 pickets, micro traxion (for simuling the second on Dark→Bonanza).
**Glacier kit:** Harness, crampons, ice axe, 1 picket each, prussiks, full crevasse-rescue setup. Three glaciers across the trip (Dome, Dark, Mary Green).
**Bivy:** Light shelter (tarp + bivy or single-wall tent), sleeping bag rated to ~25 °F, pad. Head net for Holden Pass.
**Other:** Helmet, water filter (lots of glacier melt), 1.5–2 L capacity, stove + 1 small canister for 4 days, bear can not strictly required in this zone.

## Open questions / beta gaps

1. **Dome via Chickamin Glacier crevasse state** — Riley81 2013 had to roped-pitch over a snow bridge for the final crux. Recent (within 2 years) east-side TRs would help. State varies a lot year to year.
2. **Sinister N Face bergschrund timing** — Mountaineers 2023 found it opening in late May. Riley 2013 (July 18) needed one picket. Pull a recent-year July TR before going. **If the schrund is blown, fall back to W Ridge from the col only — skip the N Face.**
3. **Schrund status on Mary Green Glacier** — Gilbertson 2018 was mid-July and it went. Check recent (within 2 weeks) TRs before going; late-season this can close out the descent.
4. **Hemlock Camp → Dark NW ridge open-forest line** — Gilbertson described it as "completely open forest." Worth pre-loading the line in CalTopo from his GPX if it's downloadable.
5. **S Fork Agnes log crossing condition** — the "first big log" is the standard. High-water years (early July, big snowmelt) can make it sketchy or wash it out. No backup beta if log is gone.
6. **CalTopo map `E8VK531` segment 2** — currently drawn as a single 11.66 mi loop from Spruce Creek. Redraw to match the Asa Mt + Icy Creek saddle + Sinister + Dome reality before going.
